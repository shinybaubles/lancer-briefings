The wayward son of a wealthy family, hoping to catch a glimpse of the wider world through his
skill as a pilot
Years living in the lap of luxury show their mark, hours spent with the best teachers money can
buy, instructing in language, mathematics, politics, and most crucially, combat. However, as the
4th born son of a political dynasty, his two options were to abandon his morals and dive into the
corrupt politicking that consumed his family, or attempt to make his own openings, at least for a
while.
As a result of his lack of real life experience, he sees lacks the intensity other pilots
possess. He sees even the bloodiest fights as honest, honorable duels where once a victor is
decided, aggression should be stopped. This has imparted him with a mirthful style can unsettle
some, who mistake his genuine enjoyment of real life combat experience as bloody minded
toying with opponents he expects to beat.
Outside of combat, his unique upbringing can lead to confusion when he seems to know
everything in some obscure legal ordinance or trade dispute, but be completely clueless about
the day to day of the ordinary man. A well constructed facade of calm politesse also hides a
mischievous playfulness that can further irritate those who find his courtly manners and rich
upbringing irritating.
