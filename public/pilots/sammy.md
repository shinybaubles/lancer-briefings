The image of a brooding mercenary. Sensibly strapped, measured, if quiet. The sole notable detail remains the
golden cross wrapped around his neck, glinting and gilded. There's always this weight on his back, in his hands, put down somewhere to rest - an altarpiece or a casket, humming ever so softly. 

He does not speak of it. 

The mannerisms of his speech escape most of his coworkers for good reason. Other than this, he's content to do his job. Fervently. Feverishly.
