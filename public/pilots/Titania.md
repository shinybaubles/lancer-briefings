A public face, known for exhilarating displays of mech prowess. 

Carrying herself with a silent stoicism that exudes a quiet sort of ferocity, 
she is straightforward almost to a fault, her blunt and concise manner of speech leaves her often at odds with those around her. Never mincing words, always steadfast. 
(Although in any promotional material you can find her at least smiling.)

Her gaze burns, most don't hold it for long. Hints of fire within, betraying a far more unbridled, wild nature underneath. 
She understands what possesses her--and at that blade's edge of understanding... exists a call to challenge.
