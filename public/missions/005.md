## Mission // #004

### In the Blink of an Eye

After being briefed on a mission to find out what had caused the recent dormancy of Clan Yuma on Sparr, the Warm Winds and Fortuna's Favored were
waylayed by a Blinkgate mishap, landing them in an unusual desert with seemingly no way out. It turns out that this event was the doing of an NHP,
one "Hugh Janus," who had intentionally brought Fortuna's Favored there in order to defeat an Evil Wizard and free his brother from imprisonment.

- Defeat the Evil Wizard
