# Karnatha, After-Action Report "Shattered Reflection" // 7.14.5014u
The OSS Warm Winds' security team was deployed to the equatorial jungles of **Karnatha** in an attempt to drive of the pirates, a plan that began with the re-taking of the mines that produce the region's filament. After securing the site for the salvage team's repair efforts, all that's left to do now is to hunt down and rout the remaining pirates.

# Karnatha, After-Action Report "Embers" // 7.20.5014u

The security team tracked the pirates to a canyon further west and fought them, alongside some raptors. After Mercedes was wrecked and the team otherwise sustained heavy damage, they decided to work out terms of the pirates' surrender - a process greatly helped along by an award-winning performance from Edward 'Bluejay' Falsax.

The pirates recovered their wreckage-trapped pilots, save for their leader - Boss Tweed, who engaged in single combat with Mercedes 'Titania' Ximena and proved victorious, leaving Mercedes heavily injured both physically and in spirit.

But with that, the pirates were routed from **Karnatha**, and the job was done. It was revealed after the fact that the pirates were, in fact, a team of mecenaries from the **Mirrorsmoke Mercenary Company (MSMC).**
