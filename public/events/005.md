# **Evil Wizard's Lair?, Blinkspace After-Action Report "Pilot Episode" // // Subjective Date 03.23.5015u - Realtime Date 01.03.5015u**
The newly-christened Fortuna's Favored embarked on an expedition to Sparr to investigate the sudden silence of Clan Yuma, but was waylaid by a Blinkgate anomaly.
Stranded in a mysterious, seemingly endless desert, the Favored fought against a horde of facsimiles - bits and pieces of Favored's adventures up until this point.
They were joined by an NHP, apparently designated "Hugh Janus," who had brought them all there in order to fight an evil wizard.

The Favored were ultimately successful in defending the shuttle, cargo bay, and the bridge of the Warm Winds in a resounding victory - although they suffered serious damage in the process.
