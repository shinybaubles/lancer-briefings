# **IPS-N Cook, Harbored in Manaraga Gate, Ural Line, Logbook Entry "Bit Off More than She Could Chew" // 10.26.5014u**
Following an offer and some taunts from *Mi-Young 'Knave' Tuite*, Samson stepped up to take on her challenge of a duel on the simulation deck.
In the few-holds hand-to-hand fight that broke out, Samson thoroughly overpowered *Tuite* and struck a decisive blow to her chin that resulted in a near-fatal fall and head injury.

*Tuite* was rushed to the medical wing by the two paramedics she had recruited to watch over the duel, but not before they sent the security team a video of the fight,
and the *Captain* of the ship made her appearance shortly after. After a confrontation with *Captain Beckham*, Samson and Jerk were able to talk down *Tuite'*s punishment,
from a black mark on promotions lasting three months, to no punishment. *Captain Beckham* relented, commenting that *Tuite* "having to eat her last meal at Port through a straw is punishment enough."
Samson, however, was tasked with cleaning up the mess left behind on the simulation deck. The party was left with a data pad for requisitioning some mission-critical resources as well.

After *Beckham* made her exit, *Vimes* let the security team know that the situation wasn't as severe as *Captain Beckham* made it out to be, and that he probably would have done the same in Samson's shoes.

The *Security Team* is left to their own devices for a few hours at the blink gate, able to get whatever metropolitan offerings of food or comfort they might want before they set out on the **IPS-N Cook's** voyage.

# **IPS-N Cook, Deep Space, Somewhere in the Ural Line, After-Action Report "Enter Sandman" // 11.03.5014u**
The Security Team was called down into Corridor 2-11B to fight off an attack, though it registered initially only as an unusual weigh-in. An engineer suspicious of the affair alerted **Trunk** personnel, and you were tasked with helping them in investigating the void spaces and connecting corridors aboard the ship.

You managed to save more than half of the engineering personnel, and were even able to avoid damage to the **Cook's** electrical conduits, ensuring a less strenuous travel for all of those aboard than expected.

After a fierce battle, the ship lurched into a *Nearlight Ejection*, losing an entire 0.9c in a matter of seconds and effectively pulverizing everything that wasn't inside of a crash cushion. The attackers took advantage of this usual strategy by outfitting their teams with technicals bearing construction foam cans. Under the immense stress, the cans ruptured and the foam spread out in the hallways of the **Cook,** practically completely halting the efforts of **Trunk Security.**

# **IPS-N Cook, Deep Space, Somewhere in the Ural Line, After-Action Report "For Whom the Bell Tolls" // 11.10.5014u**
The Security Team lent help to **Trunk**, braving the **Cook's** exterior in a bid to defend the **Fabrication Deck** from attackers that were bound for it. They faced down a team consisting of a Scout, Hornet, Sniper, and _Ronin_, the latter of which was distinguished from the others by an increased competence and religious fervor that rivaled Samson's.

Again, they triumphed, ensuring that the hostiles never reached the destination, and gathering invaluable data on the remaining attackers' whereabouts in the process. They're hiding out somewhere towards the ship's **Computation Core**. At the same  time, they acquired a case of questionably legal ammunition from the Sniper's reserves.

Samson showed mercy to the _Ronin's_ pilot, and rather than leaving him wrecked in the void of space, instead rescued the man from his wreckage and stowed him away safely.

After the combat had cooled off, the _Security Team_ was greeted by the **Warm Winds'** Chief Engineer, _Yang 'No' Wei_, and the Fabrication Deck's mixed crew of **IPS-N** and **OSS** personnel quickly got to work on salvaging the wreckage outside for filament, as well as fitting replacement parts on the _Security Team's_ mechs.
