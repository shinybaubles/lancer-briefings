# **IPS-N Cook, Harbored in Manaraga Gate, Ural Line, Logbook Entry "Bit Off More than She Could Chew" // 10.26.5014u**
Following an offer and some taunts from *Mi-Young 'Knave' Tuite*, Samson stepped up to take on her challenge of a duel on the simulation deck.
In the few-holds hand-to-hand fight that broke out, Samson thoroughly overpowered *Tuite* and struck a decisive blow to her chin that resulted in a near-fatal fall and head injury.

*Tuite* was rushed to the medical wing by the two paramedics she had recruited to watch over the duel, but not before they sent the security team a video of the fight,
and the *Captain* of the ship made her appearance shortly after. After a confrontation with *Captain Beckham*, Samson and Jerk were able to talk down *Tuite'*s punishment,
from a black mark on promotions lasting three months, to no punishment. *Captain Beckham* relented, commenting that *Tuite* "having to eat her last meal at Port through a straw is punishment enough."
Samson, however, was tasked with cleaning up the mess left behind on the simulation deck. The party was left with a data pad for requisitioning some mission-critical resources as well.

After *Beckham* made her exit, *Vimes* let the security team know that the situation wasn't as severe as *Captain Beckham* made it out to be, and that he probably would have done the same in Samson's shoes.

The *Security Team* is left to their own devices for a few hours at the blink gate, able to get whatever metropolitan offerings of food or comfort they might want before they set out on the **IPS-N Cook's** voyage.
