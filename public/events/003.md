The Security Team was called down into Corridor 2-11B to fight off an attack, though it registered initially only as an unusual weigh-in. An engineer suspicious of the affair alerted **Trunk** personnel, and you were tasked with helping them in investigating the void spaces and connecting corridors aboard the ship.

With the signal jammer having seemingly moved, _Captain Lyssa Beckham_ could finally coordinate with the **OSS** security team, ordering them to work alongside **Trunk** to drive out the remaining threat in the foam-sealed quarter of the ship, and to conduct a further investigation on how this happened.

Samson showed mercy to the _Ronin's_ pilot, and rather than leaving him wrecked in the void of space, instead rescued the man from his wreckage and stowed him away safely.

After the combat had cooled off, the _Security Team_ was greeted by the **Warm Winds'** Chief Engineer, _Yang 'No' Wei_, and the Fabrication Deck's mixed crew of **IPS-N** and **OSS** personnel quickly got to work on salvaging the wreckage outside for filament, as well as fitting replacement parts on the _Security Team's_ mechs.

# **IPS-N Cook, Deep Space, Somewhere in the Ural Line, After-Action Report "Blaze of Glory" // 12.01.5014u**
The OSS Security Team followed on a lead given by Jerk 'Offer' Lockwood, and pursued hostile forces to the IPS-N Cook Computation Deck's Core, where they encountered numerous reanimated, hostile elements that were both mechanical and biological in nature. # **IPS-N Cook, Deeper Space, Somewhere in the Ural Line, After-Action Report "Name Pending" // 12.15.5014u**
12/15:
The OSS Security Team made contact with Trunk Security Officer Mi-Young 'Knave' Tuite approximately 15 minutes after the neutralization of hostiles and reported the damage dealt to technical staff, who finished repairs to the Omni-Net node roughly an hour afterwards.

Within half an hour, the fabrication deck's recycling efforts were completed, and the Schedule-4 printers were commandeered for the sake of printing new materiel to replace the Security Team's destroyed equipment. Construction only went 3% over the energy and material budget, and early projections estimate that this will not adversely impact blinkgate construction. The Security Team helped with assembly of piecemeal components and final touches on their mechs before setting out under orders from Captain Lyssa Beckham, followed by Mi-Young Tuite, serving as rearguard.

At the communications antenna on the computation deck, the OSS Security Team encountered an Aunite threat actor self-identified as "King Solomon," who was in possession of the as-of-yet unidentified NHP casket responsible for the apparent resurrection phenomenon. According to recovered combat footage and testimonial from Officer Tuite, damage sustained by the Security Team was minimal, and the encounter lasted approximately 5 minutes, in all. Following a killing blow on a reanimated King Solomon, then controlled by an apparently cascading NHP, all apparent instances of reanimation collapsed into Greywash nanites, which have been observed to travel in "random walks," suggesting that they are under no further direction.

Investigations into the Aunite connection of this attack are ongoing.

Technical analysis from OSS Lieutenant Yang Wei revealed that the IPS-N Cook's blinkgate casket had been tampered with, preventing cycling. Its stability is not yet certain, but orders from Captain Beckham are to proceed with the mission are to inform Union authorities immediately, to finish construction of the Blinkgate, and to request a replacement casket from the Office of Interstellar Transport soon thereafter. Following the debrief with Captain Beckham, she made the Security Team on behalf of IPS-N to trade permanent printing access for select inventory in exchange for recovered technologies. Talks about this offer have been put on hold following indecision within the Security Team, though cursory examination of the technologies has revealed an apparent connection to psionics.
