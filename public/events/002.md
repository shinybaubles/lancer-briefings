# Sparr, Logbook Entry "Cold Open" // 7.27.5014u
The *Security Team* was dispatched to **Sparr** with the goal of recovering biological material from the Kaiju that's been terrorizing the **Ouray Clan**, one of the few Sparri clans with a more open policy towards outside trade and visitation.
On the **Warm Winds'** way to **Sparr**, however, the communications cut out about 3 days before the ship was meant to arrive.
After coming down to the planet, the security team encountered two stranded clansmen, one of which was Lenno - who explained that **Clan Ouray's** geothermal plant had been attacked by the Kaiju. The party decided to take them in under their protection and bring them back, along with the parts needed for repairs.
Misc Information:
 The other clans in the area are **Kiowa** and **Yuma**, the former of which is more militaristic and distrustful of outsiders, and the latter is claimed to worship machine spirits. Both possess Omni-net Nodes. 

# Sparr, After-Action Report "Metaphorical Car Keys" // 8.3.5014u
The *Security Team* encountered members of **Clan Yuma** on their way back to **Ouray's** settlement, which led to a confrontation that was defused by Samson finding common grounds in faith, and from a reasonable offer by Jerk that resulted  in the group giving up their spare parts to **Yuma** in exchange for being given passage back to Ouray.

A week before arrival, **Clan Yuma** was also attacked at their geothermal plant by the Kaiju, and it was reported that it had nested there. The eggs were destroyed by **Clan Yuma**. Samak, the **Far-Warden of Yuma** had also given the group some accounts of the Kaiju they were hunting.
It was enormous, pink, breathed fire, and burrowed through the ice on its way to the geothermal plant. It also has a regeneration factor that is halted by excessive heat. 

# Sparr, Logbook Entry "Hasty Repairs" // 8.24.5014u
The *Security Team* made its way down into the geothermal plant's magma well and discovered that the Kaiju had also made a nest in **Ouray's** geothermal region. With combined help from Edmund, Samson's NHP, and Lenno (who was borrowing Jerk's drone), Mercedes was able to repair the geothermal plant, clear the mass of eggs without rupturing any of them, and even managed to retrieve one to keep as a sample. A.valuable find, if there ever was one.

After returning to the surface with it, Chioma pointed out that the insides of the eggs seem near-identical to each other and it's likely the Kaiju underwent parthenogenesis.

Lastly, the party decided on a course of action that was suggested by Samson: To lure the *Kaiju* back towards clan **Yuma** for the sake of crippling their war effort and hopefully wearing them down into a position where **Ouray** can bargain for peace.

# Sparr, After-Action Report "Old Yeller" // 9.7.5014u
The *Security Team* attempted, and failed, to lure the *Kaiju* away from **Ouray**, and instead did battle with it within the clan's borders, finally injuring and killing it. **Ouray** breathed a collective sigh with the removal
of its threat to their safety, and quickly got to work butchering the creature for parts. **Ouray** offered to treat the **OSS** Security Team to a feast.

# Sparr, Logbook Entry "A Hero's Feast" // 10.12.5014u
Lenno lead the *Security Team* to some hot springs in order to rest, decompress, and celebrate their victory, as well as washing off remaining viscera from the encounter with the *Kaiju*. Lenno, the guide, brought the group to the forbidden springs without mentioning why it is that they were forbidden - though Jerk had an inkling of why it might be, given that the springs were, in fact, within **Yuma's** territory.

After some time bathing and relaxing, the **Far-Warden**, Samak, arrived and exchanged pleasantries - regaling the security team with the knowledge that **Kiowa** had been destroyed and was in the process of being incorporated into **Yuma's** territories. Even so, it seems that **Yuma** had lost interest in **Ouray** for now.

With admittedly soured moods and significantly less blood-stained mechs, the security team made their way back to **Ouray** for the feast, but before the events concluded they had been invited to speak with the clan's elders. It was revealed there that the eldest among the elders was, in fact, Mercedes' great-grandmother, and that it was up to her to continue the bloodline, if she so chose. More importantly, it was her duty to continue the warrior tradition of the clan, and so she was granted a family heirloom: a pneumatic hammer that was initially meant for boring tunnels in the ice which had since been repurposed for mech-scale combat.

To Samson's chagrin, most of the food on offer at the feast was various cuts of the kaiju, but there were a few mundane delicacies to enjoy. On the opposite side of the spectrum, Edmund doubled down and requested another serving of the beast's many eyes. In any case, the security team ate their fill, and were more or less concluded with their business on **Sparr** - at least for now.

# Manraga Gate, Ural Line, Logbook Entry "A Fair Price" // 10.19.5014u
The security team made good on their contract with **SSC** and delivered the egg sample to them, at which point they were congratulated by *Yuma Goto*, a higher-up within the company, in-person. They were each offered some limited access into **SSC's** catalogue to trial goods, and those who were already invested were offered further access. He also mentioned putting a good word in for the *Security Team,* which seems to have paid off..
