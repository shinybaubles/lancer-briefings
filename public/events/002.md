# Sparr, Logbook Entry "Cold Open" // 7.27.5014u
The security team was dispatched to **Sparr** with the goal of recovering biological material from the Kaiju that's been terrorizing the **Ouray Clan**, one of the few Sparri clans with a more open policy towards outside trade and visitation.
On the **Warm Winds'** way to **Sparr**, however, the communications cut out about 3 days before the ship was meant to arrive.
After coming down to the planet, the security team encountered two stranded clansmen, one of which was Lenno - who explained that **Clan Ouray's** geothermal plant had been attacked by the Kaiju. The party decided to take them in under their protection and bring them back, along with the parts needed for repairs.
Misc Information:
 The other clans in the area are **Kiowa** and **Yuma**, the former of which is more militaristic and distrustful of outsiders, and the latter is claimed to worship machine spirits. Both possess Omni-net Nodes. 

# Sparr, After-Action Report "Metaphorical Car Keys"
The security team encountered members of **Clan Yuma** on their way back to Ouray's settlement, which led to a confrontation that was defused by Samson finding common grounds in faith, and from a reasonable offer by Jerk that resulted  in the group giving up their spare parts to **Yuma** in exchange for being given passage back to Ouray.

A week before arrival, **Clan Yuma** was also attacked at their geothermal plant by the Kaiju, and it was reported that it had nested there. The eggs were destroyed by **Clan Yuma**. Samak, the **Far-Warden of Yuma** had also given the group some accounts of the Kaiju they were hunting.
It was enormous, pink, breathed fire, and burrowed through the ice on its way to the geothermal plant. It also has a regeneration factor that is halted by excessive heat. 

# Sparr, Logbook Entry "Hasty Repairs" // 9.7.5014u
The security team assisted **Ouray** with repairs to their geothermal plant, and discovered a clutch of the Kaiju's eggs in the process, successfully removing one with the combined efforts of Jerk's drone, Lenno's guidance, and
Mercedes' precise movements. Power was restored to the clan, and despite having enough biological samples to complete the mission, Samson devised a plan to hopefully better the political situation on **Sparr** by using
the Kaiju as a means of weakening **Yuma's** forces and preventing them from focusing their efforts on a hostile takeover of **Ouray.**
